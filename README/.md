Differences from my design document:
- I took the idea from the first of my two drawings provided, and ditched the second. I also did not make a security camera cut noise, and instead made the camera lose its vision cone and go dormant.
- I ditched the rage turret idea and instead with more of a Galaga feel. It would be fun to tune the turrets to make them more rage-y and make a gauntlet level, but I didn't want to die a million times in my tutorial.
- I thought I would get WAY more level design done. I said in my write up I would do 2-3 levels with 3-5 stages each and I only ended up doing three levels, or one large level with three stages.
- Most of my other ideas came through as intended from the write up.
- I also did not add an end screen, instead it just returns to the main menu.
